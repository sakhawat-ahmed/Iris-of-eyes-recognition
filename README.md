## Iris Recognition: A Comprehensive Review and Novel Approach for Wearable Devices

A research project focused on synthesizing and analyzing two key papers in iris recognition, with an emphasis on deep learning solutions for challenging real-world conditions.

## Project Overview

This project involves the in-depth analysis of a foundational survey paper and a novel research paper on iris recognition. The core objective is to understand the evolution of iris recognition technology and to critically evaluate a state-of-the-art deep learning approach designed to overcome the limitations of traditional systems, particularly in non-ideal imaging environments.

## Research Questions & Focus

The project addresses the following core academic and practical questions derived from the two papers:

•What are the fundamental stages and methodologies of the iris recognition pipeline, and how have they evolved from traditional to deep learning approaches?

•How can the critical challenge of off-axis iris recognition in wearable devices be effectively solved using synthetic data augmentation?

•What is the performance and robustness of a custom deep neural network architecture (IoU: 92.3%) when trained with synthetic data compared to traditional baselines?

•What are the key architectural, methodological, and statistical insights (ablation studies, statistical significance) that validate the proposed novel approach?

## Technical Highlights

The project includes a deep dive into the novel paper's methodology:

•Synthetic Data: Detailed pipeline for generating off-axis iris images and its dominant contribution (73.4% of total IoU improvement).

•Architecture: Analysis of the custom encoder-decoder network, including parameter count and computational complexity (2.8 GFLOPs).

•Methodology: Critical evaluation of combined loss functions (CE + Dice), optimization strategies, and regularization techniques.

•Performance: IoU of 92.3% on off-axis images, demonstrating robustness and practical feasibility for wearable devices.
